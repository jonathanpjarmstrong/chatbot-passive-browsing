The Berry-picking Principle

Users don't arrive with a fully-formed order. They forage: scan options, pick one item, let it change what they look for next. Every response should be a trailhead, not a dead end.

Design Principles

Entry
No gates before browsing
Commitment (pickup time, address) moves to checkout. The menu is free to explore.
Responses
3 trails from every answer
Deeper, lateral, or up. The user forages without composing a new query.
Psychology
Recognition beats recall
Visible options use recognition memory. A blank field demands recall. Recognition is always cheaper.
